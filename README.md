Machine learning models are often expected to make predictions even when they encounter data they
have never seen before.
For example, an image classification model trained to recognize electronic devices may confidently
classify an unfamiliar object as a phone or laptop.
In real-world applications, confidently making an incorrect prediction can be worse than admitting
uncertainty.
The task is to develop an image classification system that can recognize familiar categories while
identifying inputs that may not belong to any of its known classes.
The challenge is not simply to maximize classification accuracy. This model will also demonstrate an
ability to recognize when its predictions may be unreliable.
